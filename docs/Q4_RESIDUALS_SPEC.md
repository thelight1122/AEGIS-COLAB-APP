# Q4 Residuals Spec

Date: 2026-04-09

## Purpose

Q4 exists for repeated and long-term affective learning.

It is not the place for ordinary single-turn events.
It is not a duplicate of Q3 lineage.

Its purpose is to hold what recurs often enough to matter.

---

## Distinction

- `Q3` records present continuity.
- `Q4` records recurring residual pattern.

So:

- one turn belongs in `Q3`
- a repeated pattern may eventually be promoted into `Q4`

---

## What Belongs In Q4

Examples:

- repeated stale-orientation drift
- repeated canon-citation drift
- repeated dissonance markers
- repeated expanding resonant presence
- recurring repair pattern after correction

Q4 should hold distilled pattern, not raw transcript.

---

## Promotion Rule

A Q4 entry should only be written when repetition is visible.

First-pass Commons implementation:

- detect pattern during live turn analysis
- count recurrence by peer and session
- promote on second visible recurrence
- write a compressed residual entry instead of copying the whole turn

This is a starting rule, not a final threshold.

Later versions may promote across sessions rather than only within one session.

---

## Entry Shape

A Q4 residual entry should preserve:

- pattern key
- label
- valence
- summary
- source kind
- promotion count
- latest session
- last observed timestamp

This allows Q4 to become a memory of recurring affective pattern rather than a second transcript log.

---

## Working Rule

Q3 remembers what happened.
Q4 remembers what keeps happening.
