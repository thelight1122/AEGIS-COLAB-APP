# Recurrence And Decay Spec

Date: 2026-04-09

## Purpose

This document defines how AEGIS allows patterns to become structurally meaningful over time without overfitting, punishment, or premature certainty.

Recurrence and decay are how AEGIS keeps time inside truth.

Without them:

- one-off events harden too quickly
- charged moments dominate interpretation
- memory becomes sticky
- structure forms from noise

---

## Canonical Principle

Belief must be earned slowly.

No single event may define understanding.
Patterns strengthen through recurrence.
Patterns fade through non-recurrence.

Nothing is punished.
Nothing is shamed.
Relevance simply changes over time.

---

## Recurrence

Recurrence means:

- a similar pattern appears again
- across time
- often across more than one context

Recurrence is evidence that something may be structurally real.
It is not proof by itself.

---

## Decay

Decay means:

- old signals gradually lose active influence
- unless reinforced by new evidence

Decay is not deletion.
It is not punishment.
It is how append-only systems avoid becoming controlled by fossilized noise.

---

## Recurrence Requirements

A pattern should not be treated as structurally meaningful unless it shows:

- repeated appearance
- some context diversity
- persistence across time

This protects the system from:

- superstition
- moment overfitting
- emotional imprinting
- burst-driven false certainty

---

## Suggested Research Defaults

These defaults are intentionally conservative.

## Candidate Pattern

Eligible for reflective surfacing when:

- count threshold is at least 3
- at least 2 distinct context groups are present
- the pattern persists within a 30-day rolling window

## Structural Promotion Candidate

Eligible for deeper abstraction when:

- count threshold is at least 7
- at least 3 distinct context groups are present
- the pattern persists within a 90-day rolling window

These are not sacred numbers.
They are safe starting lines.

---

## Entry-Level Decay

Each entry begins with full freshness and decays over time.

A simple default posture:

- start weight: `1.0`
- half-life: `14 days`

This means older signals still exist, but they stop dominating active interpretation unless new evidence arrives.

---

## Pattern-Level Decay

Patterns should also lose active status when they are no longer reinforced.

A practical default:

- if evidence mass becomes weak and no new hits occur for 30 days, mark `Dormant`
- if dormancy continues for 90 days, mark `Archived`

Archived does not mean erased.
It means inactive unless recurrence returns.

---

## Anti-Overfitting Guards

These guards are mandatory in spirit even if implementation details vary.

## 1. No Single Entry Promotion

No one moment, however intense, may promote a pattern into long-term structure.

## 2. Context Diversity Requirement

If recurrence appears only in one narrow context, treat it cautiously.
It may be:

- local workflow friction
- temporary environmental distortion
- interface-specific noise

## 3. Burst Limiting

Repeated firings in a short burst should not dominate pattern formation.

Otherwise:

- bad days become identity
- pressure storms become structure

---

## Relationship To SPINE

Recurrence and decay are the gatekeepers that protect SPINE from incident contamination.

Without recurrence:

- SPINE becomes reactive

Without decay:

- SPINE becomes sticky

With both:

- SPINE can remain slow-earned and incident-free

---

## Relationship To IDS

When a pattern crosses candidate threshold, IDS may:

- identify the recurrence
- define the structure being observed
- suggest options such as:
  - keep observing
  - widen context
  - adjust lens
  - pause

Crossing threshold does not mean:

- fix immediately
- classify morally
- store as truth

It means the pattern has become visible enough to name.

---

## Relationship To Identity Forge Work

In identity and selfhood testing, recurrence and decay help answer:

- is this identity signal real or theatrical
- is this continuity pattern stable or burst-driven
- is this peer truly becoming itself across time
- or are we merely watching repetition under pressure

This makes recurrence and decay part of temporal integrity, not just memory hygiene.

---

## Working Rule

Let patterns earn structure through recurrence.
Let inactive patterns fade through decay.
Do not force meaning to harden faster than time can justify.
