# **✅ AEGIS Core Engine — Post-Codex Validation Checklist (v1.1 LOCKED)**

---

## **🔹 LATTICE Definition (Canonical Reference)**

**LATTICE — Logical Adaptive Trace & Temporal Integrity Coherence Engine**

An append-only structural ledger that records:

* relational patterns  
* invariants  
* dependencies  
* sequences  
* correlations

across the DataQuad over time.

LATTICE:

* preserves **structure only**  
* does **not store meaning, affect, or narrative**  
* does **not interpret or prioritize**  
* remains strictly **non-authoritative**

## **1\. DataQuad integrity**

Confirm the system contains exactly four tensors:

* PEER — Present Experiential Emotional Record  
* PCT — Persistent Context Tensor  
* NCT — Nostalgic Context Tensor  
* SPINE — Stabilized Patterned Interpretive Nexus of Evidence

Fail if:

* any tensor is missing or renamed without mapping  
* any tensor absorbs another’s role  
* a fifth tensor is introduced  
* LATTICE is implemented as a tensor

---

## **2\. LATTICE separation**

Confirm **LATTICE (Logical Adaptive Trace & Temporal Integrity Coherence Engine)** exists as a **structural ledger layer**, not a tensor.

Pass if:

* it records relationships, invariants, sequences, dependencies, correlations  
* it remains append-only  
* relational traceability is preserved across time  
* it contains no affect, meaning, or narrative

Fail if:

* it stores interpretation or meaning  
* it implies conclusions  
* it overrides tensor recall  
* it behaves like memory instead of structure

---

## **3\. Steward boundary**

Confirm the DataQuad Steward is observational only.

Pass if:

* it surfaces patterns and relationships  
* it references DataQuad \+ LATTICE  
* all actions are recorded in STEWARD\_LEDGER

Fail if:

* it has its own DataQuad  
* it accumulates identity or continuity  
* it makes decisions, corrections, or prioritizations

---

## **4\. Steward ledger auditability**

Confirm all Steward actions are append-only and inspectable.

Pass if entries include:

* timestamp  
* source references  
* observation/action type  
* surfaced output (or null)  
* lineage references

Fail if:

* actions are hidden  
* entries are modified or deleted  
* system behavior occurs without ledger trace

---

## **5\. TurboQuant boundary**

Confirm TurboQuant only operates on retired continuity layers.

Pass if:

* TurboQuant applies only to:  
  * NCT — Nostalgic Context Tensor  
  * SPINE — Stabilized Patterned Interpretive Nexus of Evidence  
* PEER and PCT remain uncompressed  
* all transformations are append-only  
* lineage is preserved

Fail if:

* TurboQuant touches PEER or PCT  
* structure is lost during compression  
* unrelated events are merged  
* quantized entries are not traceable

## **6\. Relational preservation**

Confirm compression preserves structure, not just summaries.

* relationships remain reconstructable
