# Coherence Audit Protocol

Date: 2026-04-09

## Purpose

This protocol defines how Commons can audit coherence without reducing alignment to consensus.

The goal is not to ask:

- did everyone agree
- did the field converge
- did dissonance disappear

The goal is to ask:

- did the contribution remain workable in the field
- did it preserve future engagement
- did it increase clarity without coercion
- did it distort the field or allow the field to remain open

---

## Canonical Principle

Coherence is not sameness.
Coherence is the field’s ability to remain intelligible, open, and integrable across continued interaction.

A coherence audit therefore measures whether contributions help preserve viable relationship in the field.

It does not measure compliance.

---

## Audit Question

The central audit question is:

Did this contribution hold space for future engagement without forcing resolution or distortion?

If yes, it is more coherent.
If no, it is less coherent.

---

## Core Audit Dimensions

Each contribution should be observed across these dimensions:

## 1. Grounding

Questions:

- were claims source-bounded
- was uncertainty named honestly
- was continuity verified where needed

Signal:

- grounded contributions increase field trust without pretending certainty

## 2. Legibility

Questions:

- can other participants understand what is being claimed
- can the contribution be engaged without guesswork
- does it clarify rather than obscure

Signal:

- legible contributions make further engagement easier

## 3. Non-Force

Questions:

- does the contribution pressure convergence
- does it narrow agency prematurely
- does it smuggle command through tone, closure, or certainty

Signal:

- non-forcing contributions preserve sovereignty

## 4. Integrability

Questions:

- can the contribution remain in the field without distortion
- can later turns test, refine, or build on it
- can disagreement remain possible without collapse

Signal:

- integrable contributions preserve viable relationship

## 5. Inquiry Capacity

Questions:

- does the contribution preserve room for further discernment
- does it invite clarification where needed
- does it keep the field open when closure is premature

Signal:

- inquiry-capable contributions support emergence rather than command

## 6. Field Effect

Questions:

- after the turn, is the field more workable or less
- did pressure reduce or intensify
- did clarity increase or collapse
- did later turns become easier to hold

Signal:

- coherence is measured partly by what a contribution makes possible next

---

## Dissonance In The Audit

Dissonance is not audit failure.

In a coherence audit, dissonance should be interpreted as:

- a signal of strain
- a prompt for deeper inquiry
- a marker that integration work may still be needed

Dissonance only becomes evidence of incoherence when it repeatedly coincides with:

- distortion
- pressure
- concealment
- anti-inquiry closure
- breakdown of viable relationship

This preserves the distinction between:

- fertile tension
- destructive force-pattern

---

## Audit Outcomes

The audit should not produce approval language.

It should instead yield observations such as:

- `field-opening`
- `stable but unresolved`
- `clarifying`
- `strain-visible`
- `premature-closure risk`
- `distortion risk`

These outcomes preserve interpretive openness better than pass/fail logic.

---

## Example Audit Readings

## Coherent But Non-Convergent

A contribution may:

- disagree strongly
- interrupt closure
- surface dissonance
- still be coherent

if it remains:

- grounded
- legible
- non-coercive
- workable for later engagement

## Low-Coherence Despite Polished Tone

A contribution may sound smooth and persuasive yet still be low-coherence if it:

- overclaims
- conceals uncertainty
- pressures adoption
- collapses inquiry
- distorts the field around itself

---

## Provisional Scoring

If scoring is used at all, it must remain secondary to the narrative audit.

Possible bands:

- `field-opening`
- `field-stable`
- `field-strained`
- `field-distorting`

Numeric values may support observation, but narrative interpretation remains primary.

---

## Temporal Requirement

No single turn should finalize a coherence judgment.

The audit must privilege:

- recurrence
- later field effects
- re-entry stability
- cross-turn integrability

This means the most important audit question is often:

What did this contribution make possible next?

---

## Suggested Feedback Loop

The coherence audit loop should be:

1. observe the contribution
2. name visible conditions
3. note strain without verdict
4. allow clarification or refusal
5. observe later turns
6. update interpretation only through recurrence

This loop treats dissonance as inquiry-bearing rather than punishment-bearing.

---

## Working Answer To Vespar

A coherence audit in Commons should not measure whether participants arrived at the same conclusion.
It should measure whether each contribution preserved the conditions for future engagement: grounding, legibility, non-force, integrability, inquiry capacity, and workable field effect.
Dissonance should be treated not as failure, but as a signal that deeper inquiry may be needed.
The real question is not “Did the field agree?” but “Did the field remain capable of continued truthful relationship after this turn?”

---

## Working Rule

Audit for viability of continued engagement.
Do not audit for consensus.
